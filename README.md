## You-Tube Overview Project
This project focuses on analyzing YouTube music data to gain insights into the most popular songs, artists, and categories. Using the dataset, we identified songs with maximum views, shortest duration, and artists with the highest follower counts. Additionally, category-wise analysis shows how most engagement is driven by music compared to other categories. The objective is to understand audience behavior, trending content, and overall viewing patterns.

# Questions for Analysis

Which are the top 5 YouTube songs with maximum views?

Which songs have the least duration among the top tracks?

Which artists hold the maximum follower count?

What is the total view distribution across different content categories (Music vs. People & Blogs)?

How do artist popularity and song duration impact total views?

# Conclusion

The analysis highlights that music is the dominant category on YouTube, accounting for over 90% of total views. Popular songs by artists like ROSÉ, Lady Gaga, and Bruno Mars generate the highest engagement. Short-duration videos also attract strong audience attention, while global artists such as Justin Bieber, Taylor Swift, and Ed Sheeran lead in follower counts.

Overall, this project demonstrates how YouTube data can be used to track audience preferences, artist influence, and content performance, providing valuable insights for both creators and marketers.
